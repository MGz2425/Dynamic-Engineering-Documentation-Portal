H.E. Metropolitan Pachomius of Beheira, Matrouh and Pentapolis

Early Life and Education:
Metropolitan Pachomius was born on December 17, 1935, in Shibin El Kom, Monufia, Egypt. He graduated from the Faculty of Commerce at Ain Shams University in 1956 and later studied at the Coptic Theological Seminary, graduating in 1959.

Monastic Life:
He joined the Monastery of the Virgin Mary (El-Sourian) in the Nitrian Desert. He was ordained a monk on November 11, 1962, taking the name Father Antonios El-Souriany. He was later ordained a priest in 1966 and elevated to the rank of Hegumen in 1968.

Episcopate:
On December 12, 1971, he was consecrated as the first Bishop of the newly created Diocese of Beheira, Matrouh, and Pentapolis by H.H. Pope Shenouda III. He was later elevated to the rank of Metropolitan on September 2, 1990.

Locum Tenens:
Following the departure of H.H. Pope Shenouda III in March 2012, Metropolitan Pachomius served as the Locum Tenens (Acting Pope) of the Coptic Orthodox Church. He oversaw the transition period and the selection process that led to the enthronement of H.H. Pope Tawadros II in November 2012.

Legacy:
Known for his profound wisdom, administrative skills, and spiritual fatherhood, Metropolitan Pachomius has been a pillar of the Coptic Orthodox Church for decades. He established numerous churches, service centers, and monastic communities within his diocese and beyond.
